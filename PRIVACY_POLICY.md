# Privacy Policy — App Cloner

_Last updated: 2026-05-30_

App Cloner ("the app") is designed to clone other applications installed on your
device. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app
accesses and how it is handled.

## Data we collect
App Cloner does **not** collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All clone
configurations and settings are stored **locally on your device** and are never
uploaded to our servers (we do not operate any backend servers for this app).

## On-device access
To function, the app accesses the following on your device only:
- The list of installed applications (to let you choose which app to clone).
- The APK files of apps you choose to clone (to create the clone).
- Local storage (to save clone records and temporary files).

This information never leaves your device.

## Advertising
The free version of the app displays ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may
collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and may use a device advertising
identifier to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in
your device settings. Premium users do not see ads.

## Purchases
Premium upgrades are processed by **Google Play Billing**. We do not receive or
store your payment details; these are handled entirely by Google.

## Google Play Services
Some cloned apps rely on Google Play Services. The clone may prompt you to sign
in with a Google account; that interaction is between you and Google and is
governed by Google's privacy policy.

## Permissions
- `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` — list installed apps to clone.
- `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` — install cloned apps.
- `REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES` — uninstall clones.
- `INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — required by the ads SDK.
- Storage — read/write clone files.

## Children's privacy
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
in the "Last updated" date above.
